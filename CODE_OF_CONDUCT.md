# Code of conduct

Help people use and improve the project respectfully. This applies in project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces explicitly managed for the project.

Be considerate of different experience levels, backgrounds, languages, and accessibility needs. Give specific technical feedback, explain disagreements, and respect a maintainer's decision to narrow or decline work.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not post customer data or credentials, including in examples or screenshots.

Report conduct concerns privately to [klikgefixt@gmail.com](mailto:klikgefixt@gmail.com), including context and relevant links when safe. For a software vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled with discretion; information may need to be shared to investigate or protect participants. No response-time guarantee is made.

The owner may edit or remove content, request a change in behavior, close a discussion, or restrict participation according to the situation. Explain the reason where safe and practical, and allow the affected person to provide context privately. Good-faith reports must not trigger retaliation.
