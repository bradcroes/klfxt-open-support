# Changelog

## Unreleased — 0.1.0 alpha

The package version is `0.1.0`; no public release is asserted. The owner approved source publication and Apache-2.0 on 2 November 2026. See the release checklist for actual verification and publication status.

### Implemented locally

- NL/EN intake and customer tracking with a bearer capability.
- Single-owner desk with status, priority, assignment labels, public replies, internal notes, customer directory, and queue totals.
- React/Vite UI, Hono Worker API, D1 migrations, isolated local setup, and verification commands.
- Optional owner email outbox, disabled by default; approved hosted booking/payment links only.
- Resolved-ticket retention controls, contribution and security policies, and validation-only CI.

### Deferred

Staff accounts and roles, multiple businesses in one deployment, attachments, automatic customer email, inbound mailbox handling, payment API/webhooks, booking synchronization, and customer capability recovery/rotation are outside v0.1. They are not announced releases or commitments.
